In a world where software engineers spend most of their time planning and reviewing coding agents, the most impactful way to ship more is to get faster at planning and review.

Vibe Kanban is built for this. Create workspaces where coding agents execute, review their changes, and merge the results. Everything runs on your machine, with no account needed.

To plan work with kanban issues and collaborate with your team, connect Vibe Kanban to a [self-hosted server](/self-hosting/overview).

We're not a coding agent, but work seamlessly with a handful of the most popular options like Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and OpenCode.

```bash
npx vibe-kanban
```

One command. Describe the work, review the diff, ship it.
