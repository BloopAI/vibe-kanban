<Note>
This guide needs a self-hosted Vibe Kanban Cloud server. Vibe Kanban no longer uses the hosted cloud. To set one up, see [Deploy with Docker Compose](/self-hosting/deploy-docker).
</Note>

A self-hosted cloud lets you collaborate with your team in real-time. Your projects and issues sync to your server, so everyone sees the same projects, issues, and updates.

<Info>
**Already using Vibe Kanban locally?** Cloud features use the same interface you're familiar with. The difference is your projects and issues sync to your server and you can invite team members.
</Info>

## Step 1: Launch Vibe Kanban against your server

Start Vibe Kanban with `VK_SHARED_API_BASE` set to the public URL of your server. You don't need to rebuild Vibe Kanban.

```bash
VK_SHARED_API_BASE=https://vk.example.com npx vibe-kanban
```

The application opens in your browser at `http://localhost:...`. For Windows, remote access and other options, see [Connect Vibe Kanban to your server](/self-hosting/overview#2-connect-vibe-kanban-to-your-server).

## Step 2: Sign In

To access Cloud features, you need to sign in.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click Login">
  Click the **Login** button in the sidebar or when prompted.
</Step>

<Step title="Choose a sign-in method">
  Select one of the sign-in methods your server is configured for:
  - **Sign in with GitHub** - Use your GitHub account
  - **Sign in with Google** - Use your Google account
  - **Email and password** - Use the bootstrap local login (`SELF_HOST_LOCAL_AUTH_EMAIL` and `SELF_HOST_LOCAL_AUTH_PASSWORD`) set on the server

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/cloud/sign-in-dialog.png" alt="Sign in dialog with GitHub and Google options" />
  </Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Authorise the application">
  If you chose GitHub or Google, you'll be redirected there. Sign in if needed, then click **Authorize** or **Allow** to grant Vibe Kanban access.

  <Info>
  Vibe Kanban only requests access to your basic profile (name, email, avatar). It cannot access your private repositories or post on your behalf.
  </Info>
</Step>

<Step title="Return to Vibe Kanban">
  After authorising, you're automatically redirected back and signed in.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Your Personal Organisation

When you sign in for the first time, a **personal organisation** is automatically created for you, along with an **Initial Project** to get you started. This is your private workspace.

<Info>
Personal organisations are just for you - you cannot invite other members. To collaborate with a team, create a new organisation (see Step 5).
</Info>

## Step 4: Create Your First Project

Now create a project to organise your work.

<Steps>
<Step title="Click New Project">
  Click the **+ New Project** button in the sidebar.
</Step>

<Step title="Enter project details">
  - **Name** - What you're working on (e.g., "Mobile App", "Website Redesign")
  - **Colour** - Pick a colour to identify the project

  <Frame>
    <img style={{maxHeight: "300px"}} src="/images/cloud/create-project-dialog.png" alt="Create Project dialog with name and colour fields" />
  </Frame>
</Step>

<Step title="Click Create">
  Your project is created with a kanban board ready to use.

  <Frame>
    <img src="/images/cloud/project-kanban-board.png" alt="Project kanban board with default columns" />
  </Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>

## Step 5: Invite Team Members (Optional)

Create a team organisation to collaborate with others, then invite as many members as you need.

<Steps>
<Step title="Invite team members">
  Open Organisation Settings (click the gear icon next to your org name), then:
  - Click **Invite Member**
  - Enter your teammate's email address
  - Select their role (**Member** or **Admin**)
  - Click **Send Invitation**

  <Frame>
    <img style={{maxHeight: "300px"}} src="/images/cloud/invite-member-dialog.png" alt="Invite Member dialog" />
  </Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>

If your server has email configured (`LOOPS_EMAIL_API_KEY`), they'll receive an email with a link to join your organisation.

## Signing Out

To sign out:
1. Click your **profile icon** in the bottom of the left sidebar
2. Click **Sign out**

<Frame>
  <img style={{maxHeight: "350px"}} src="/images/cloud/user-menu.png" alt="User menu showing Sign out option" />
</Frame>

Your data remains on your server. Sign in again anytime to continue where you left off.

## Local Workspaces and Cloud Projects

Workspaces always run on your machine. Projects and issues live on your server:

| Local workspaces | Cloud projects and issues |
|------------------|---------------------------|
| Data stored on your machine | Data synced to your server |
| Only you can access | Team members can collaborate |
| No sign-in required | Requires sign-in |
| Works offline | Requires a connection to your server |

Both appear in the same Vibe Kanban interface.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="I can't sign in">
**Possible causes:**
- Pop-up blocker preventing the OAuth window
- Network connectivity issues

**Solutions:**
1. Disable pop-up blocker for localhost
2. Check that your server is reachable at the URL in `VK_SHARED_API_BASE`
3. Try a different browser
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I don't see Cloud features">
**Possible causes:**
- `VK_SHARED_API_BASE` isn't set, so Vibe Kanban hides sign-in and all cloud features
- You're not signed in

**Solutions:**
1. Restart Vibe Kanban with `VK_SHARED_API_BASE` set to your server's URL, in the same shell that runs `npx vibe-kanban`
2. Click **Login** in the sidebar
3. Complete the sign-in process
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My teammate can't see our projects">
**Possible causes:**
- They haven't accepted the invitation
- They signed in with a different email

**Solutions:**
1. Check if the invitation is still pending in your Members settings
2. Resend the invitation
3. Verify they're using the email address you invited
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
